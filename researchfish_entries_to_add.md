## Engagement Activities / Impact

### Kenilworth Revealed public AR heritage app

- Suggested Researchfish section: Engagement Activities or Impact Summary
- Year: 2025
- Form of engagement: Public engagement through an augmented-reality cultural heritage app
- Description: MyJAMS Ltd contributed ARME-derived immersive media technology to Kenilworth Revealed, a free augmented-reality heritage app launched by Kenilworth Town Council on 10 November 2025. The app uses AR plaques, video, sound, and interactive quests to help visitors explore the history of St Mary's Abbey in Abbey Fields.
- Impact: The work demonstrates how technical methods developed around ARME can support public heritage, tourism, local storytelling, and cultural access beyond the original virtual ensemble rehearsal use case.
- URLs:

### Phase correction in violin ensemble performance

- Suggested Researchfish section: Publications
- Publication: Scientific Reports
- Date: 2026
- DOI: 10.1038/s41598-026-46205-6
- Authors: M. S. Li, D. Ward, R. Stables, W. L. Chua, J. Howe, K. A. Quinn, A. M. Wing
- Description: The article investigates how violinists use auditory timing cues in ensemble performance. It uses a linear phase correction model to examine how performance mode and rhythmic complexity influence synchronisation with a violin duo.
- URL: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-46205-6
